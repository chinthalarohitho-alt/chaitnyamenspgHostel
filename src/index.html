1. Timings & Curfew

  • Gate closes at 10:30 PM sharp daily.
  • Late entry: Prior pass via manager message.
  • Night-out: Parent permission required.

2. Visitors & Guests

  • Lounge hours: Open 24 hrs — visitors welcome any time.
  • Room privacy: No outside guests inside bedrooms.
  • Parent stay: Guest room upon advance notice.

3. Substance-Free Campus

  • 100% Smoke & Alcohol Free environment.
  • Zero-Tolerance: Strict deposit forfeiture on breach.
  • Healthy & Secure community for all residents.

4. Cleanliness & Quiet Hours

  • Quiet hours: 11:00 PM – 6:30 AM.
  • Room upkeep: Cooperate with daily housekeeping.
  • Wall care: Use soft-boards; no drilling or nails.

5. Electrical Appliances

  • Allowed: Laptops, mobiles, tablets, study lamps.
  • Prohibited: Heavy immersion coils & heaters.
  • Power saving: Switch off appliances when away.

6. Rent & Notice Period

  • Rent due: On or before the 5th of every month.
  • 30-day notice: Required before vacating.
  • 100% Refund: Deposit returned within 3 days.
